Bees
Bees are the core of your hive in The Majestic Bees. Each bee has a unique rarity, color type, and pollen preference. Collect them through eggs, level them up with items, and unlock more hive slots as you progress.

Hive Slots

You start the game with 10 hive slots. Every 5 player levels you gain 1 additional slot, up to a maximum of 50 slots. XP to level up is earned purely through progression.

Obtaining Bees

Bees are obtained by hatching eggs. Each egg type hatches a specific set of rarities.
Egg Rarity Pool Notes
Basic Egg Common only Always hatches a Basic Bee
Silver Egg Rare, Epic, Legendary No Common bees
Golden Egg Epic, Legendary No Common or Rare bees
Diamond Egg Legendary only Always hatches a Legendary bee

Bee Levels

Each bee has its own level that can be raised by feeding it Treats or Fruits. Leveling up a bee increases its pollen collection rate, conversion speed, and the level of enemies it can attack.
Item XP Given
Treat 20 XP
Fruit 50 XP

The exact XP formula required per level is currently unknown.

Bee Colors

Every bee belongs to one of three color types. Color affects which flowers a bee is most efficient with, as well as the nature of its special abilities.
Color Best Flower Description
Colorless White flowers Performs best on white flowers.
Red Red flowers Specialized in red flowers.
Blue Blue flowers Specialized in blue flowers.

All bees can visit flowers of any color with equal frequency. However, when a bee collects from a flower matching its own color, it gathers significantly more pollen per visit. A bee's special abilities also synergize with its color type, such as area boosts and pollen bombs that exclusively benefit their matching flower type.
