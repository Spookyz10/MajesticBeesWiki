Mobs
Mobs are hostile creatures found throughout the world of The Majestic Bees. Defeating them rewards you with useful items, essences, honey, and rare drops. Each mob has its own location, difficulty, and loot pool.

Mob List

Drop chances marked with ✦ are under 5% and are boosted by your Loot Luck stat, the higher your Loot Luck, the better your odds on these rare drops. Other drops always roll at their listed chance regardless of Loot Luck.
