Flower Effects

Effect Works on Description
Fire All Flowers Burns the flower. Red Flowers give 2x more pollen when burned.
Ice All Flowers Multiplies the pollen gained. Has weaker effect outside of Blue Flowers
Obsidian All Flowers Instantly converts pollen gained from flowers with this effect into honey.
Soul Fire All Flowers 3x more pollen compared to regular fire.
Milk All Flowers Converts 40% of the pollen gained from flowers with this effect into honey and grants a movement speed buff.
