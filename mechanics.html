Mechanics
How flower effects work and what each one does to your pollen.

Flower Effects

Effect Works on Description
Fire All Flowers Gathers the burning flowers every 1.5s while it lasts. Red flowers give x5 pollen, Blue and White give x2.5. Boosted by Fire Pollen and Fire Duration.
Soul Fire All Flowers Stronger fire: Red flowers give x16 pollen, Blue and White give x3 every 1.5s. Boosted by Fire Pollen and Soul Fire Pollen.
Ice All Flowers Pollen from iced flowers is boosted by Ice Pollen, but only on Blue flowers. Ice and Fire on the same flower turn into Obsidian.
Frost All Flowers Like Ice, but multiplied by Frost Pollen. Adding Ice to a Frost flower keeps it as Frost.
Obsidian All Flowers All pollen from the flower is instantly converted into honey. Boosted by Obsidian Pollen on White flowers.
Blossom Obsidian All Flowers Obsidian created by Blossom Rain. Also boosted by Blossom Pollen.
Milk All Flowers 30% of the pollen gathered is instantly converted into honey. Coconut Smoothie milk also gives +25% Movespeed and x1.45 Obsidian Pollen for 15s.
Poison All Flowers Gathers poisoned flowers every 1.5s at x30 pollen for its duration (14s from Poison Harvest on the Nature Belt).
Heaven All Flowers Upgraded tiles that gather 8 pollen x Heavenfall Pollen every 1.5s for 15s, with 25% instantly converted. Heavenfall stars landing on them gather a 5x5 patch.
Paint (Red / Blue / White) Matching color Flowers of the matching color give x2 pollen. Comes from paint passives and Splatter Bee.
