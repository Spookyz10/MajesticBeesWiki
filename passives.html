Coconut Smoothie

Every 15 coconuts

Drops 10 splashes of Coconut Milk. Coconut Milk has 30% Instant Conversion. When stepping on Coconut Milk, receive Coconut Smoothie Boost for 15s (+25% Movespeed and x1.45 Obsidian Pollen). Activates Every 15 Coconuts.

Equipment: Coconut Bag

Equipment: Sunny Helmet

Giant Weed

Break 50 vines (3 min cooldown)

Break 50 vines in the field where your Pumpkin Sprinkler is placed and a Giant Weed grows there for 2 minutes. Stay near it to link and receive Giant Weed Link (+25% Instant Conversion, +50% White Pollen, +50% Bomb Pollen, +10% Movespeed, +5% Bee Ability Rate, +25% Pollen). Cooldown: 3 minutes.

Equipment: Pumpkin Sprinkler

Equipment: Gourd Spider Helmet

✦

Time Stop

Timekeeper tokens (2 min cooldown)

Collecting a Timekeeper Bee token has a 1% chance (+0.5% per Timekeeper token since the last activation) to stop time for 7s. Your screen turns grey, enemies are slowed by 50% and you receive Time Stop Boost (+50% Movespeed, x0.5 Bee Movespeed, +100% Tool Speed, x2 White Pollen, x2 Bomb Pollen). All pollen collected during Time Stop is instantly converted into honey when it ends, and all tokens collected activate at once. Cooldown: 2 minutes.

Equipment: Time Capsule

✦

Time Stream

Every 120 seconds

Every 2 minutes in a field, a golden point appears. Reach it in time to gain a Time Stream stack and spawn the next point with 0.5s less time. Miss a point and you lose a stack. Smaller fields give less time. Time Stream (stacks 10x): +2% Movespeed, +1.5% Instant Conversion, +7.5% White Pollen, +5% Bomb Pollen per stack. Lasts 5s per stack.

Equipment: Timekeeper Mask
