Passives

Equipment passives, activation requirements, and effects.

Coconut Smoothie

Every 15 coconuts

Drops 10 splashes of Coconut Milk. Coconut Milk has 30% Instant Conversion. When stepping on Coconut Milk, receive Coconut Smoothie Boost for 15s (+25% Movespeed and x1.45 Obsidian Pollen). Activates Every 15 Coconuts.

Equipment: Coconut Bag

✦

Sun Eclipse

Every 80 seconds

Creates 4 Sunny Beams, which gather Pollen (Multiplied by Solar Pollen). Additionally, receive Sun Eclipse Boost for 20s (x1.5 Pollen) & All players in the field receive Sun Eclipse Dual Boost for 20s (x1.05 Pollen & x1.1 Capacity Bonus) .Activates Every 80s.

Equipment: Sunny Helmet

Coconut Harvest

Every 20 seconds

Creates 3 Coconuts, which gather Pollen and covers flowers in Milk. Milk has 30% Instant Conversion. Activates Every 20s.

Equipment: Coconut Belt
