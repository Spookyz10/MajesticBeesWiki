About the Shop

The Shop is a wooden building located near Black Bear. It has one door at the front and two large windows. It is surrounded by trees and bushes, giving it a cozy and welcoming look. The outside has a few wall lights that keep the area bright. Inside you can find 5 tools, 4 bags, and 1 of each type of gear.
