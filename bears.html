Bears

Bears are NPC quest-givers scattered across the world of The Majestic Bees. Each bear offers a series of progressively challenging quests, complete them to earn rare eggs, Honey, and exclusive aura boosts.

Each bear is tied to a specific zone and has a unique set of quests. Quests reward you with items like Honey, Treats, Royal Jelly, and sometimes rare Eggs.
