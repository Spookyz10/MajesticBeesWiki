Hive
Your hive is the heart of your colony in The Majestic Bees. Customize it with Skins and Stickers to change its appearance and gain permanent stat bonuses. Expand it by purchasing more slots with honey.

Hive Slots

You start the game with 5 hive slots. More slots are purchased with honey, up to a maximum of 50 slots.

Skins

Skins change the appearance of your hive and grant passive bonuses. Each skin belongs to a rarity tier.

Stickers

Stickers are cosmetic decorations that also provide stat bonuses. Most are obtained randomly while gathering or through special events.
