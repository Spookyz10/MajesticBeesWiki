Falling Apples

Apple reward pools, new food, and apple worms introduced in Update 11.

Falling Apples takes place in Apple Field. When an apple falls, it spawns an Apple Worm or a Golden Apple Worm.

The event was introduced in Update 11. Apple gives 250 XP when fed to a bee; Golden Apple gives 2,500 XP.

Normal and Golden apples have separate reward pools, listed below.

Chances are calculated automatically from each reward pool and shown per selection, before luck modifiers. Percentages are rounded; multiple rolls can give different overall results.
