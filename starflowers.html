Starflowers
Starflowers can be placed in any field. Each rarity lasts a different amount of time: Basic lasts 3 minutes, Rare lasts 4 minutes, Epic lasts 5 minutes, Legendary lasts 7 minutes, Mythic lasts 8 minutes, and Lunar lasts 4 minutes. When placed, the game rolls to decide the actual tier that spawns. This means even a Basic Starflower can turn into a Mythic one. Bees break them by collecting pollen nearby; they require different amounts of pollen to destroy them. Every time a token drops, the game rolls both the field color pool and the All Fields pool at the same time. The Bamboo Field uniquely drops Bamboo instead of the standard White pool.
