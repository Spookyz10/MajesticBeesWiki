Art Hall and Painter Bear

The Art Hall arrives with the 30 Bee Zone. Canvy, also known as Painter Bear, has five reworked quests involving stickers, pollen, and Sticker Canvases. Her Inspiration aura adds 5% Energy Bonus per stack, with a library cap of 100 stacks.

Paint equipment

Canvy’s Extra Paintbrush uses Colorful Splash every 20 swings. Canvas Helmet uses Sticker Hunter every 360 seconds. Paint Bucket uses Art Splashes every 30 seconds. The update announcement calls these Paint Brush, Paint Helmet, and Paint Backpack.

Oscar and the Oscar Shop

Oscar is a bunny with five quests involving the nearby fields and ingredients. Her Oscar Shop sells Seed Pouch, Haywalkers, Gardening Gloves, Trinket Belt, Farmer’s Guard and Scarecrow Guard for Honey and rare garden materials.
